`DerivativeExplorer` combines `CalculusEngine.derivative` with the `TangentLine` component. As the `x₀`
slider moves, the tangent line and the slope update live.

**Preview** is the lab itself; **Code** shows where the slope is computed and how the tangent is
drawn.
