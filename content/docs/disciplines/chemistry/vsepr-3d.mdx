## Bond sticks

In this example the bonds are only marked. Placing a cylinder between two atoms requires a rotation that aligns the cylinder's local Y axis with the bond direction. That is done on the `three` side with a `quaternion`, not with bindings; the [3D Scene](/docs/concepts/three) page can be consulted as a starting point.

## Limit

Only the geometry is shown. Electron-pair repulsion, bond-length differences, and steric effects are not modeled.
